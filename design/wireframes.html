ludo Wireframes

Grayscale sheets for every screen of Ludo Unbiased: local pass-and-play, Wi-Fi and hotspot multiplayer with 45-second reconnection, and AI seats. The board is drawn from the same geometry tables the Flame board will use. Red numbers on a sheet match the list beneath it; blue pencil explains behaviour.

Decisions to make

  • Card position on 320 dp screens. Recommended: keep the four corner cards at 140 × 64 in a compact form (small symbol avatar and dice, about 6 letters of name). Alternative: one strip of four cards above the board, which gives names more room but breaks the card-beside-yard link.
  • Dice on the card or in the centre. Recommended: on the active card, as drawn. Every player always looks at the same place for their own turn. A centre dice would cover the HOME triangles.
  • Choosing a token from a mixed stack. Recommended: tap moves your first movable token in the cell; long-press opens a picker with only your tokens. Other colours on a safe cell are never selectable.
  • "Replace leavers with AI" default. The spec says off: a seat empty for 45 s is removed. On would keep 4-player games at 4 players.
  • Timer number or ring only. Recommended: ring only, plus a number for the last 10 s. The ring alone is hard to read precisely; a constant number adds visual noise to every turn.
